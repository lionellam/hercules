Loading screen

An experimental feature. With its switch on, opening or reloading Hercules shows a white screen with the ship picture and the name Hercules, until the sign-in has been checked and the Log screen's figures have arrived. It then fades into a finished Log screen. With the switch off, nothing changes. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new.

Behind the loading screen, the Log screen builds as it does today, grey placeholders included. Switching tabs, or months on Summary and History, still shows the placeholders.

Press a button to replay. The loading times are pretend, to show each case.

6. The rules

WhenWhat happens
The switch is off (the starting point on every device)Nothing changes: "Loading…", then the Log screen with its grey placeholders
The switch is on, and the app is opened, reloaded, or you've just signed inThe loading screen shows. The switch is read from the device before anything is drawn, so "Loading…" never shows first
Switching tabs, or months on Summary or HistoryNo loading screen. The grey placeholders show, as now
Everything arrives in under half a secondThe ship stays for half a second, then fades out
After 3 seconds and still loading"Still loading..." appears under the name
After 10 seconds, offline, or loading failsThe loading screen gives way to the Log screen, which shows its grey placeholders, its slow note or its offline message as it does today, so the app never stays stuck on the ship
Dark modeThe loading screen stays white (Option A)
Reduce Motion is onIt disappears without fading
Before the page arrives (for example, the home-screen app starting)A blank screen for a moment, as now. Not part of this change
