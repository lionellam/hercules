Settings page

Mockups for a new Settings tab. Categories moves into Settings and is renamed "Categories & budget". Settings also takes in the light/dark choice and Sign out, which today sit on other screens. Everything about adding, editing, budgets and retiring categories stays exactly as it is now. Blue dashed outlines mark what's new or moved.

Option A: Settings as a menu

Settings is a short list. Tapping "Categories & budget" opens it as its own page, with "‹ Settings" at the top to go back. The tab bar keeps four tabs, with Settings (a gear) in place of Categories.

Option B: everything on one page

No second page: Settings shows the categories list directly, between Appearance and Account. Fewer taps to reach a category, but a longer page to scroll.
