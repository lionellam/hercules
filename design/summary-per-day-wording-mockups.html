Summary's daily figure: "from tomorrow"

Option B for choice 1. Summary's figures stay the same; only the words "from tomorrow" are added after "/day", so it's clear the figure covers the days after today, unlike the Log screen's budget for today. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new.

The example: day 25 of 30 (Wednesday 25 November), with some spending already logged today. Each category is in a different state, so every kind of note is shown. On the last day of the month, Summary already says "$X left today" instead of a daily figure, so that day doesn't change.

Longer lines: the notes now run to two lines more often. Phones 375 and 360 pixels wide are shown, the common narrow sizes.
