Sheet animation

Tap Open to show a sheet and Close (or the sheet's own buttons, or the darkened area) to dismiss it. On a phone the sheet slides up from the bottom and eases to a stop, while the background fades in; closing slides it down a little faster. On wider screens the centred dialog fades in and grows slightly instead. Confirmation sheets ("Discard your edits?", "Delete this expense?" and so on) move the same way.
