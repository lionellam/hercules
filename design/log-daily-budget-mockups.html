Today by category: spending against today's budget

On the Log screen, each bar under Today by category shows today's spending against today's budget for that category, instead of its share of today's spending. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new.

Today's budget = (monthly budget − spent this month before today) ÷ days left in the month, counting today. It stays the same all day. Summary doesn't change.

The example: Wednesday 25 November, day 25 of 30, so 6 days are left counting today. One category in each state:
