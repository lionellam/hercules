Light and dark switch on the GitHub Pages

One Light | Dark switch, the same on all four pages: the home page, the user guide, App Screens and Expense Flow. It works like the one on Expense Flow today: it starts from the device's setting, and the selected option shows in the soft green. New: the choice is remembered in the browser, so picking Dark on one page keeps the others dark too, until it's changed.

Placement (option A, desktop and phone): the top of the page, on the same row as "← Hercules home": the way home on the left, the switch on the right. The home page has no way home, so the switch sits alone at the top right. Dashed blue outlines mark the switch.
