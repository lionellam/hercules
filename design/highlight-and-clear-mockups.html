A yellow "Budget not set" line, and clear buttons on the cards

Two usability changes. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new.

1. Making "Budget not set for this category" stand out

Three ways to add yellow. All use a soft yellow with dark amber text in the light theme, and a deep amber with light yellow text in the dark theme, so the text stays easy to read in both. The app's red stays for over budget only; yellow reads as "worth a look", not as a warning.

Option A, dark theme, and with the bubble open

Other places with an icon like this

The only other one is the "?" beside "Not included: USD 12.00", on Log (under today's total), Summary (under the month's total) and History (while searching). It explains that spending in other currencies isn't counted. I suggest leaving it as it is: it's information, with nothing for the person to do, whereas the budget line asks them to act. Keeping yellow for "something you can do" keeps it meaningful. Both are drawn below for comparison.

2. A clear button on the cards' text fields

Today, replacing a merchant, remark, name or budget means deleting it character by character. Proposal: a round grey × (the same look as the clear button in the Log screen's text box) that empties the field in one tap and leaves the cursor in it, with the keyboard still open, ready for new text.

Which fields: Merchant and Remarks (review and edit expense cards), Name and Monthly budget (category card), and the amount. Not Category or Date, which are picked from a list or a calendar, and not the Name of "Other", which can't be changed.

Option A (recommended): the × appears while you're typing in a field

Fields look as they do today, with their pencils. Tapping a field with something in it puts the cursor there, as today, and its pencil turns into a ×. Tapping the × empties it. This is how clear buttons usually work on phones, and it keeps the cards calm: one icon per line, and only the field you're in shows a ×.

Option B: a × on every field that has something in it

One tap clears a field without tapping it first. But each filled field then shows a × instead of its pencil, so a card mixes pencils and ×s, and a × sits one tap away from wiping a value by accident.

Questions for you

  1. The yellow: A, a pill around the line (recommended: it stands out and still reads as part of the row); B, a full-width strip (strongest, but heavier: with several categories without a budget, Summary gets busy); or C, yellow text with no background (lightest).
  2. "Not included": leave it as it is (recommended), or give it the same yellow?
  3. The clear button: Option A, only while typing in a field (recommended), or Option B, on every filled field.
  4. The amount: include it (drawn in A), or leave the amount without a clear button?
