Burn-up chart sizing

Proposals for making the burn-up chart on Summary larger and easier to read on a phone. Each mockup uses the app's own colours, with sample data for Food & Dining ($500 budget) on 7 November, day 7 of 30. Nothing in the app has changed.

The measurement under each mockup is the share of the screen's width used by the plot itself: the area where the lines are drawn.

Step by step

Two small changes, one at a time. Everything else stays as it is now: the chart's height, the "$210.00" label on today's dot, the "Budget 500" label and the grey panel's padding.

Steps 1 and 2 in other situations

The same two changes in the awkward cases: a one-off payment on day 1, going over budget, late in the month, and dark mode with the pointer resting on 5 November.

Proposal B, keeping the amount beside the dot

Proposal B's sizing (no side margins, edge to edge, a little taller) with the "$210.00" label kept on today's dot. The label sits just above and to the right of the dot while there's room, and moves to its left near the end of the month. The taller chart always leaves room above the dot, so the label never needs to drop below it.

Near the end of a month that runs over

One month where Food & Dining spending runs ahead of its $500 budget, shown on four days near the end. Watch the "$" label, "Today" and "Budget 500" move as the line crosses the budget.

Further options: side by side

Today, the left and right edges of the chart are reserved for labels: "0" and "500" on the left, and the "$210.00" end label on the right. Both proposals remove those margins: the budget amount appears only in the "Budget 500" label, and the end label is dropped because the amount is already shown above the chart.

Proposal B in other situations Recommended

Checking that the remaining labels stay clear of the lines in the awkward cases: a one-off payment on day 1, going over budget, late in the month, and dark mode. A pointer is resting on 5 November in one of them to show the tooltip.

What changes in Proposals A and B

  • No y-axis labels. The "0" is the baseline and the budget amount moves into the "Budget 500" label at the right end of the budget line. Exact figures are still in the tooltip and "Daily figures".
  • No "$210.00" label on the end dot. The same amount is shown just above the chart ("210.00 of 500.00"), and late in the month the label would run into "Budget 500".
  • "Budget 500" moves out of the way. It sits at the right end of the budget line, and moves to the left end when today's dot gets close to it late in the month.
  • Date labels hug the edges. "1 Nov" is left-aligned and the last day is right-aligned, so neither needs spare margin.
  • Slightly taller: 200 instead of 172, so the steps are easier to tell apart.
  • Proposal B only: the chart also runs to the edges of the grey open-category panel, using its 12-pixel padding on each side.
  • Early in the month the spending line still covers only the first part of the chart, because the chart shows the whole month. That's what lets the dashed line show the full budget.
