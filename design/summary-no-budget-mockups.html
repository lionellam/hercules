Summary: categories without a budget

Today, a category without a budget still gets a bar on Summary, but it isn't measured against anything: it's drawn relative to the month's biggest category. Shopping at 640.00 shows a third-full bar, which reads as "a third of a budget used". This proposal removes that bar and points people to setting a budget. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new; a blue ring marks what was tapped.

Feasibility: all of it can be built with what the app already has. The "i" and its bubble reuse the "?" beside "Not included" on Summary (same size, same tap area, closes when you tap elsewhere). The Edit category card is the existing one. The only new piece of plumbing is letting Summary open that card for a given category; it is straightforward.

Decisions so far

  1. Where the link goes: Option B. The card slides up over Summary, as the app's cards do, with nothing selected; after Save it closes and the row gets its bar, "of", day tick and pace line.
  2. Past months: no bars. Every category shows its name and amount only, as past budgets aren't recorded.
  3. Retired categories: name and amount only, with no bar and no line below.
  4. The tap target: the whole "Budget not set for this category" line opens the bubble; the "i" turns dark while it's open.
  5. The link: "Set a budget for" and the category's name.
