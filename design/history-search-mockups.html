History search

Mockups for a search field at the top of History. The list filters with each letter you type; an "×" on the right clears the field; and "No search results" shows when nothing matches. These use the app's own styles and example expenses; nothing in the app has changed.

Blue dashed outlines mark the new search field.

1. Searching the month on screen

The field sits in the pinned top section, between the heading and the month switcher, so it stays in view while the list scrolls. Matching words are highlighted, and a line above the list counts the matches and adds them up.

What it searches:
  • The merchant, the words you typed, the category and the remarks, ignoring capitals: "grab" finds "Grab".
  • Amounts too: "14.20" finds $14.20 expenses.
  • Every word must match, in any order: "grab airport" finds a Grab ride with "airport" in it.
Switching months keeps the search, so you can step back month by month looking for the same thing.

2. Alternative: searching all months

The same field, but results come from every month, newest first, under a heading for each month. The month switcher hides while searching. This needs a new search on the server, so results appear a fraction of a second after you stop typing, rather than on every letter.

To decide

  • This month or all months. Option 1 is instant and needs no server change; to look further back you switch months with the search kept. Option 2 finds anything in one go but responds just after you pause typing.
  • The total line ("3 results · $52.80") is optional; it's handy for questions like "how much did I spend on Grab this month?".
  • Leaving History clears the search, so the next visit starts with the full list.
