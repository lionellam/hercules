Centred Sign out, and pencils on the category sheet

Two small changes. Dashed blue outlines mark what's new; the dashed circles on the Settings frames show roughly where each thumb reaches comfortably when holding the phone in one hand.

1. Sign out, centred

Option A (recommended): the same red "Sign out" row, with its text centred. The whole row stays tappable, edge to edge, so it's easy to hit with either thumb, and it still looks like the rows above it. Option B: a centred, outlined red button. It stands out more as a separate action, but it's a smaller target and a new style the app doesn't use elsewhere in Settings.

2. Pencils on the Edit category sheet

A pencil to the right of the Name and Monthly budget values, as on the review and edit expense sheets. Tapping the pencil puts the cursor in that box, as it does there. The built-in "Other" category can't be renamed, so its Name shows no pencil (its budget still can be changed). Add category gets the same pencils.
