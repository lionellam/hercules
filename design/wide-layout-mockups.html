Wide screen layout

On tablets and computers the app is capped at 820 pixels wide and centred, so a laptop window leaves wide empty bands on either side. These mockups show the Summary screen in a 1,440-pixel laptop window and a 1,024-pixel iPad held sideways, now and with two options. They use the figures from your screenshot (7 November, dark mode). Nothing in the app has changed.

The dashed pink lines mark the edges of today's 820-pixel layout, so you can see how much each option widens it.

  • What stays the same: the phone layout, the bottom tab bar, the two columns on Log and Summary, and the 32-pixel side padding inside the app.
  • Option A widens the app in two gentle steps. Everything grows a little, including the burn-up chart, whose text grows with it (about 25% larger at 1,000 wide). That stays comfortable to read.
  • Option B uses more of the window and gives the extra width to the category list and chart. At 1,120 the chart would be about 75% wider than on a phone, and its text would scale up with it unless the chart's labels are changed to keep a fixed size. That's more work, so B would need that change as well.
  • Both are CSS changes to the existing wide-screen rule. History and Categories are single lists, so they'd get longer lines too; I'd cap their lists at the current width if that reads better.
