Notes

  • Typing isn't held up. In every option the text box and Parse work straight away; only the list and "Spent today" wait.
  • Only when there's nothing to show yet. The placeholders appear only before the first load. Later refreshes, after saving or editing, keep showing the current list as they do now, so nothing flickers.
  • History and Summary have the same wait the first time they're opened. The same treatment could be used there, as placeholder rows on History and placeholder bars on Summary, either now or later.
  • Reduce Motion: the shimmer, spinner and moving line stand still.
  • Why it's slow after a quiet spell: most likely the hosting and the database both start up again after being idle, as they do on free plans. This makes the wait clear; it doesn't make it shorter.
